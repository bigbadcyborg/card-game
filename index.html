How to Play

Goal

Reduce your opponent's Life from 20 to 0.

Resource: Signal

Generated by tapping Nodes. Play one Node per turn.

Turn Structure

  1. Untap all your cards
  2. Draw a card
  3. Main phase: play Nodes and cards by paying Signal
  4. Combat: attack with untapped Companions
  5. End turn (hand limit 7)

Card Types

  • Nodes — Tap for Signal
  • Companions — Creatures that attack and block
  • Actions — Instant spells
  • Bonds — Ongoing effects

Combat

Attackers tap. Damage is simultaneous. Companions die if damage ≥ Toughness.

Digital duel

Play on one screen and pass the device between turns, or start a LAN table so each player uses their own browser on the same network. Hands stay hidden either way.

  • Life starts at 20. Signal crystals increase by 1 each turn, up to 10, and refill on your turn. Unspent Signal does not carry over.
  • The first player starts with 3 cards and does not draw on turn 1. The second player starts with 4 cards.
  • Play one Node from hand each turn. Tap a Node for 1 extra Signal this turn.
  • Companions cannot attack, or use tap abilities, on the turn they enter unless they have Haste. Choose your Companion, then choose an enemy Companion or hero. Both deal damage at the same time.
  • Actions resolve immediately. Bond Denial sets a Secret that counters the next enemy Action.
  • Protector stops enemy Companions with power 1 or less from attacking your other Companions.
  • Ally Network triggers when you play an Action, before that Action resolves.
  • Hand limit is 7 at the end of your turn. Drawing from an empty deck deals increasing fatigue damage.
